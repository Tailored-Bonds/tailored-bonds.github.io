💡 How to Use the 1XLR8 Bond Structuring Assistant

1XLR8 is Tailored Bonds’ AI Structuring Engine — trained on decades of bond data, SEC filings, and placement structures. It helps you design realistic, investor-ready bond proposals from simple project ideas.

  • Start Simple: “We have 30 oil wells in Angola.”
  • Ask for Scenarios: “Show 3 bond structures with different coupons and tenors.”
  • Iterate: “Now make it suitable for European ESG investors.”
  • Refine: “Add reserve accounts and covenants.”
  • Paste Data: You can input real project summaries or PPM sections — 1XLR8 will extract facts and tell you what’s missing.

⚠️ All outputs are for research and scenario analysis — not investment advice.
